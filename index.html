Our Culinary Heritage / আমাদের ঐতিহ্য

Serving Genuine Flavours at the Heart of Dhaka

দ্যা সিলভানা রেস্টুরেন্ট — ঐতিহ্য ও খাঁটি স্বাদের মেলবন্ধন

Situated at 10, Shahbagh Biponi Bithan, Dhaka-1000, The Sylvana Restaurant is proud to be a warm culinary landmark in Dhaka. We bring together time-honoured Bengali cooking traditions, authentic Old Dhaka spices, and contemporary gourmet dining under one welcoming roof.

From our early morning flaky Parathas with rich Dim Bhuna or Bhaji, to our midday aromatic Mutton Kacchi Biryani and comforting Alu Bhorta with Rui fish curry, every dish is prepared with fresh local ingredients, genuine care, and uncompromised hygiene.

✔ Authentic recipes rooted in traditional Bangladeshi culinary craft
✔ 100% Halal fresh meat, poultry, and daily-sourced local produce
✔ Welcoming family environment, student-friendly rates, and express takeaway
